Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated: 2026-06-10

繁體中文

HOLOCARD META 是非營利的粉絲專案，不販售商品、不投放廣告、不要求註冊帳號。

  • 分析工具：本站使用 Google Analytics 4 統計瀏覽情況（瀏覽頁面、點擊的牌組／卡片、語言等）。GA 會使用 Cookie 並產生匿名識別碼。
  • 同意機制：在你於同意橫幅按下「接受」之前，GA 不會載入、不會寫入任何分析 Cookie。你可隨時透過側欄的「Cookie 設定」變更選擇。
  • 本機儲存：語言偏好、同意狀態與安裝提示的關閉時間僅儲存在你的瀏覽器（localStorage），不會傳送到任何伺服器。
  • 第三方內容：卡片與牌組圖片載入自外部網站（hololive-cardgame.github.io、holocardstrategy.jp、官方網站）。載入圖片時，你的 IP 位址會傳送至該等網站。
  • 聯絡方式：請透過 GitHub Issues 與我們聯繫。

English

HOLOCARD META is a non-commercial fan project. No products are sold, no ads are served, and no account is required.

  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site usage (pages viewed, decks/cards opened, language). GA sets cookies and generates pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Consent: GA is not loaded and no analytics cookies are written until you press "Accept" on the consent banner. You can change your choice anytime via "Cookie settings" in the sidebar.
  • Local storage: Your language preference, consent choice, and install-prompt dismissals are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent to a server.
  • Third-party content: Card and deck images are loaded from external sites (hololive-cardgame.github.io, holocardstrategy.jp, the official site). Your IP address is sent to those hosts when images load.
  • Contact: Reach us via GitHub Issues.
